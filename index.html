Visa desk

We never collect or store passport numbers, scans or dates of birth. Real e-visas are issued only by governments. LotusLane opens the official portal and gives you a packing checklist.

Checklist before you apply

  • Passport valid 6 months beyond entry (most of Asia).
  • Blank page or two for stamps if not fully electronic.
  • Digital photo: white background, recent, JPEG under 2 MB.
  • Onward / return ticket and hotel address for first nights.
  • Proof of funds if asked (bank screenshot or cards).
  • Yellow-fever certificate only if you arrive from a risk country.

Extension reality check

Extensions are done in-country at immigration, not by this app. Typical tourist extensions: Indonesia VOA +30 days at immigration; Thailand via visa / DTV for long stays; Vietnam 90-day e-visa is the simple long option for many passports.

Open official portals

Arrival cards (not visas) you still need: Thailand TDAC, Malaysia MDAC, Singapore SGAC, Philippines eTravel, South Korea K-ETA for some passports.

Vegan Asia field guide

Look for these marks and phrases. Tap HappyCow for live maps.

Thailand — เจ (jay)

Buddhist vegan: no meat, fish sauce, dairy, garlic or onion. Chiang Mai and Bangkok are dense and cheap ($1.50–4 a plate). Vegetarian Festival each autumn.

HappyCow Thailand

Vietnam — chay

Ask for phở chay, bún chay, cơm chay. Ho Chi Minh City is one of the fastest-growing vegan scenes in the world. Watch nước mắm (fish sauce).

HappyCow Vietnam

Taiwan — certified vegan logo

Highest vegan restaurant density in Asia with a national TFDA vegan mark. Taipei is the easiest “eat anything on the street” city for plant-based travellers.

HappyCow Taiwan

India — green dot ≠ vegan

Huge vegetarian culture. Say “no ghee, no paneer, no curd, no honey.” South India (Bengaluru, Kerala) and Rishikesh are especially easy.

HappyCow India

Indonesia — tempeh heartland

Tofu and tempeh everywhere. In Bali, Ubud/Canggu are packed with vegan cafés. Watch terasi (shrimp paste) in sambal.

HappyCow Indonesia

Japan — dashi trap

Tokyo and Kyoto have excellent vegan ramen and shōjin ryōri. Outside big cities, assume dashi (fish stock) unless you ask.

HappyCow Japan

    Money, ATMs, water, health

    • Notify your bank. Wise / Revolut usually beat airport desks. Decline DCC (dynamic currency conversion) at ATMs.
    • Thailand/Vietnam/Indonesia: ATMs often add 220 local + your bank fee. Take larger withdrawals.
    • Japan and Korea: IC cards (Suica / T-money) for trains. Cash still useful at tiny shops.
    • Drink bottled or filtered water in most of South and Southeast Asia. Ice is usually machine-made in tourist towns.
    • This is not medical advice. Check your doctor for hepatitis A, typhoid, and Japanese encephalitis if you will be rural for weeks. Carry ORS sachets and a simple first-aid kit.
    • Scooter: licence + helmet. Many policies refuse claims without both.
    • Tap water is generally fine in Singapore, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, and much of Georgia’s cities.
